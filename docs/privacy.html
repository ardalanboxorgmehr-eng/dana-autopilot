Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Dana Autopilot is a private tool used only by the Instagram account @danestani.ruzz (دانستنی روز) to manage its own account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Publishes posts that the account owner has prepared to @danestani.ruzz.
  • Reads comments left on @danestani.ruzz posts.
  • When a comment contains the keyword named in the post's caption, sends that person one private message with the material the post promised, and a short public reply under the comment.

What information is used

Only what Instagram provides for comments on our own posts: the comment ID, the comment text, the commenter's username, and the time. The comment ID and username are stored so the same person is not messaged twice. Nothing else is collected.

What we do not do

We do not sell, share or rent this information, use it for advertising, or combine it with any other data. We never message anyone who did not comment the keyword.

How long it is kept

Records of sent messages are kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate messages, and no longer than 90 days.

Deleting your data

To have your information removed, send a direct message to @danestani.ruzz on Instagram asking for deletion. We remove it within 30 days. See also the data deletion instructions.
